Welcome to Family Chart!

Family Chart is your intuitive portal for organizing, preserving, and linking your lineage across generations. Designed to streamline your genealogy research, FamilyChart lets you easily build detailed individual profiles, edit vital details, and filter through living and deceased family members by location. Whether you are adding new relatives or mapping out complex connections, FamilyChart brings your family history together in one clear, searchable workspace.
